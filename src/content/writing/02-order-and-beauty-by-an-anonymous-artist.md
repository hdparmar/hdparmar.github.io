title: Order and beauty by an anonymous artist
order: 2
kind: Note

As I stand at the crosswalk, I see many people passing and cars going by but that is not what I wanted to see. I wanted something else, something that would have some meaning, something that captures the transient nature of life. And I saw this person perched atop on a ladder, his brush dancing across the window frames in brilliant white strokes. His concentration was unwavering, his movements very well practiced and precise. Amongst all this chaos, there is a sense of quiet purpose in his work. It is as if he is bringing a touch of order and beauty to the world. As the green light takes me forward, I can’t help but feel a sense of admiration for this anonymous artist, quietly transforming the mundane into the extraordinary.
