Was tsunami a scream at people out of despair, regret.
And do you think the sea burdened itself with the insane amount of corpses it holds.
Do you think river ever longs for human soul floating on it.
Do you think ocean passionately sings to people on the shore.
Do you think sea misses people sometimes.
Do you think oceans can be lonely sometimes.

Sargasso Sea doesn’t have a shore.
bounded by four currents; In Atlantic Ocean.
An Ocean gyre.
