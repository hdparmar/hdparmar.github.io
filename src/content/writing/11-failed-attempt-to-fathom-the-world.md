title: Failed attempt to fathom the world
order: 11
kind: Poem
photo: sun-on-water

When I looked up to the night sky
engulfing Baltic Sea, I felt time is cruel
but the stars shined brighter than ever
It gave hope to pain stricken heart
And strength to the empty body
The soul woke up when I saw the shooting star

maybe it was you, that I got a glimpse of you,
momentarily, I realise it now Baa.
Maybe it was you, who signalled me to be strong and happy
Just as you always do.
This was the same place I watched the star flew
In the time due
You devote your life selflessly
Cracked skin of your palm recited stories
Unfathomable, amount of love it has touched
Unbearable have you left
You would want us to shine
And spread warmth like the sun
And be kind like the moon
And love as big as the ocean
I didn’t get to hug you but
I will spread my arms and feel my soul touch yours
When I see stars shine as bright as you.
