---
title: listen, if I am the moon
order: 10
kind: Poem
photo: night-water
---
I don't look into your eyes
because I know your pupils are the event horizon
and it will suck me in
into nothing
and everything

and I would not want to get out
but I will have to
to tell the world what I found inside your eyes

but then if I did get out
I wouldn't want to tell the world what I saw
because I want to keep it to myself

I don't fear falling into abyss
but I fear
I will not come out
ever again.
