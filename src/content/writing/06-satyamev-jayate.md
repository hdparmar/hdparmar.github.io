There is a saying in India, Satyamev Jayate, which means, Truth alone triumphs. But looking at the world I think the information that is true hardly comes above surface. Truth can be both harmful and necessary. When it comes to people not everyone has the caliber to handle blunt truth. Maybe because it is easier that way, without truth there is less pain.

Don’t you think the world would be a better place if everyone was slightly more honest? It is a paradox. Humanity has learned to deny truth more merely than to face the truth.
