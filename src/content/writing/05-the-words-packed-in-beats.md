I admire everything about music. Sounds and lyric. Yes, it is absolutely a leap towards a modern experience of poetry. Many upcoming indie folk artists have become so good from lyrical stand point, they are enchanting deep poems.

And also how I can miss the prose of rhyming in rap. The words packed in beats of hip hop. In Rap songs vivid stories have been told. Absolute beauty. Artists get only 16 bars to squeeze words in. And it is not enough but they have done so in an exceptional way. With single syllable rhymes to multi syllables to cross beat to double entendre to switching rhyming lanes between beats.
