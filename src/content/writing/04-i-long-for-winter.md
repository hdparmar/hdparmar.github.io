I long for winter. I long for a warm perfect blend coffee above the snow covered streets. A velvety glossy foam with steamed milk and espresso. I long for the books I haven’t read. I long for a house in the dense forest of Norway. I long for such lavishness I can’t have. But most of all, I long for smiles on loved ones. I long for the smile on my mother’s face when I call. I long for happiness in sad people. I long for open mindedness in people. I long for the perfect curry I can make. I long for chicken shawarma from Midan at 2 am. I long for conversations after midnight. I long for a thesis project in Barcelona. I long for a stable job. I want a perfect 10-hour good sleep.

All the people that I meet in the darkness of night seems to struggle with what their heart is desiring. I long for peace in this war stricken world. The longing for love. For feelings that stings the most. The moments that brings shivers down the spine. The regret of over things; desires that could have been something else. Eternal sunset, Starry skies, rainy days with thunder. I’m lost in longing for impossible things from a lucid reality.

Importantly, I long for a perfect haircut. All the barbers that I go to seems to be having a bad day; messes up my hair. haha. And a perfect watermelon.
