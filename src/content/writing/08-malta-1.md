I have left the compass
and the maps
and still the sea has found my land
unbearable this fleeting waves
that softly kisses my shore and leaves without notice
just how your kisses left without notice

I have made caves in the heart of my land, where the sea slides softly, just like your memories
they are unaware of whatever is happening on the land

can my land bear your sea of memories?

I have become like a sailor, learning the patterns of waves, and stretching the marks of sea where depth is expected.

I have learned that it is not in my fate to find my beloved for can this sea ever engulf this tiny land in the middle of nowhere.

but still I stand still with the strong sharp cliffs and sniper’s nest and telescope to be on the lookout for my beloved.

But I have learned that I am not of the thinking that one can find love by searching, so you will find cliffs gone grey & sniper’s nest abandoned, like it’s spider’s nest now.

But even if you are able to find me, I am afraid I would have been lost & dry, for I would have disintegrated into your sea, for your waves have weathered my land for centuries.

Then I would be the one would live inside you, and you will be the one who will go on a search of a land like me.
