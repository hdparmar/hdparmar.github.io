I don’t know how to begin writing this excerpt, because I am thinking in silence, I think. then I start writing, how impressive is the language, I think, and so much frightening that it doesn’t quite do the job fully, that it fails to express the inexpressible. Now how can I say something which is brewing up in my heart, in the silence it is growing, so I put on thinking of a place by the war on drugs and when Adam sings, “it was back in little bend that I saw you”, I begin writing again

I think that what is real love? what does it mean by loving a person? are we loving our idea of that person or are we actually loving the person? then I can’t think anymore further, and then adam sings, “love is like a ghost in the distance out of reach”, I think yes, that is it, it is out of reach, but no, it is within us, how can it be out of reach? and then adam sings “Once I had a dream”, then I remember the dream I had

we were together, outside, somewhere, in a cafe maybe, or walking I don’t remember the place, I remember i held back myself a little, maybe more, because i feared you won’t like me, and then you said you feared the same, and i think that is the fundamental flaw isn’t it, why are we so self-conscious that we want to make that person like us? but then i say let’s go with the flow and you look at me with a smile and you agree, and then i can’t stop looking at your eyes, i think that’s why i don’t remember the place but your eyes, now the hi-hat in the song is waking me up, I wake up and i think, and i think my the relationships grow in the spaces and the silence between people, because that’s where the magic happens, because that’s when we think of that person a lot more, we form our ideas, we reminisce about our time together. Words were dead, the moment they are said, and my yearning for you was higher than words so I remain silent for a bit more. the great silence that has birthed from my dreams and I will not wake from your silence.

I then stop writing, I can’t think more but the song has ended but I replay it, now, i put it on loop and wait for the adam to sing, “see it through my eyes”, but in me waiting for this moment I miss it, if we wait for something to happen, we miss it. and then I am standing on the shore, the river flowing, and you are on the other side of the shore, so that is what silence should be, let it be the river between your souls.

and then adam sings, “see it through my eyes, walk me to the water” and I think i should write about when i looked into your eyes, maybe a poem,
