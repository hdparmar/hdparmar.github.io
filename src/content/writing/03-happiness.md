Happiness to me is eating tasty food, eating ice cream outdoor when it is -1 degrees, taking a walk with friends in forest, going to falafel kungen at 2 am and saying bror what’s upppp while one who serves recognises me and smiles genuinely; who adds extra spicy sauce without asking me. Happiness is to make my people happy.

I don’t really know how to define happiness generally but happy moments certainly has power to make even the shallowest void of grief disappear; at least for a moment. Happiness is the moment you live in present, where your heart does not worry and mind doesn’t have clue for past and it fleets in future.
