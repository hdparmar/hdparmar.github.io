Well, I went for a walk, with just tea and me and existence!

I was walking walking and watching the thoughts pass by, and hearing the birds chirp, so many birds chirping, some close and some distance, some I saw flying, what a flight that is, some just barely grazing the surface of the water and breezing past against the wind, as I watched it fly by I saw it diminish into a point or as if it has landed in the water, then I start to recognise my thoughts that one is a memory, yes, it brings a smile on my face and the other one is a conversation that never happened but I thought that if it would happen and I would want it to go like this, but then I recognised that too, what a devilish trick mind plays to keep itself going, but wait a moment, there is another thought of how I want the product to come out, this is neither a memory or a conversation or a desire or not necessarily desire but I think one must give something back to existence, it has given us this beautiful life, we must give something back as a gift, it is nudging me saying create this and I like it, I want to create it.

Then I sip the tea, I feel the warmth of it as it pass through my body, tea brings such joy and look at the water, sometimes the wave were rough and sometimes smooth, so beautiful, maybe the waves also go through motions in life, in its journey.

Then I decide to continue walking, as I walk I recognise the bark of the trees, what a phenomenal mosaic by nature, awestrucking, such level of beauty without any artist, it is shaping on its own accord, as if it has surrendered to nature and nature has took care of it, some barks are plain white and some just brown.

It was at this moment that I realise that, it would be foolish to go away from the world, foolish, and it would be foolish to keep asking for things, instead, we must give, we are so full already, so full, I assure you, we will go to parties and mingle but don’t go chasing connections, we will go to museums but not forcing ourselves to like something, but instead watch what it brings to us, yes, we are open, we must be open, we should be able to recognise things that happen to us, things that will make us more aware, aware of the world around us, then actions will start happening not because we are asking for something, or we want people to listen or watch or hear, but because we have something to say, something to give, something to show, this kind of action doesn’t ask for anyone’s permission, doesn’t ask for anyone’s validation, it simply gives them a chance to experience something, if not, so what? they don’t owe anything to you, neither you owe anything to them, but you must realise you owe something to existence.

Yes, we will have to put on some performance sometimes, yes, we will have to use our ego sometimes, yes, we will be angry, sad, happy, joyful sometimes, but it is in the recognition of these things that will ultimately bring joy, when you recognise it is pain, you enjoy the pain, not in a bad way but only just by recognising you say Ahh what a moment, it is bringing me pain, what an immensely powerful feeling that it, and it may bring tears to you, and then sudden joy!

You’ll then realise you will have to keep on walking, keep on walking, wherever it leads you, whatever it might be, own it, own the goddam situation, the world is looking for you, it is not you who is looking for the world, because the world is right in front of you, there is no need to look, just recognise what is useful to you in silence and use it.
